Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and, when analytics cookies are used, understand how visitors interact with the content.

inesgrimaux.com does not load any non-essential cookie until you make a choice on the cookie banner shown on your first visit. Your choice ("Accept" or "Reject") is remembered locally on your device (via localStorage), which is not itself a tracking cookie.

If you accept, the Site loads Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, which set cookies to distinguish visitors and measure traffic (e.g., pages viewed, session duration, and general location at country or city level). No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used.

These cookies are managed by Google LLC in accordance with Google's own privacy and cookie policies. Ines Grimaux does not control how Google processes this data beyond the aggregated reports made available through Google Analytics.

You can change your choice at any time using the "Manage Cookies" link in the footer of every page, which reopens the consent banner. You can also delete cookies already stored, or block them entirely, through your browser settings.

If you reject cookies, the Site continues to function normally — no feature depends on analytics cookies. Only the ability to measure aggregated site usage is affected.
